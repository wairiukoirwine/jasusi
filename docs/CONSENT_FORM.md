# Message donation — consent text (draft for supervisor / ethics review)

> This is a draft. It must be approved by your supervisor (and the ethics committee if required)
> before any message is collected.

## For the Google Form introduction
**JASUSI research project — donate a scam message**

I am a final-year student at Africa International University building a tool that detects scam SMS
and emails in Kenya. You can help by donating messages you have received.

- **What I collect:** the text of the message, whether the sender was a phone number or a short name
  (for example "MPESA"), and the month you received it.
- **What I do not collect:** your name, your phone number, or anything that identifies you. Please do
  not include them.
- **Anonymisation:** before a message is saved, phone numbers, names and ID numbers in it are replaced
  with placeholders such as `<PHONE>` and `<NAME>`. This is automatic and may miss some names, so
  please also remove any you notice.
- **Use:** the messages are used only to train and test the detection model and to write the project
  report. Anonymised examples may be quoted in the report.
- **Storage:** messages are stored securely, are not shared publicly, and are deleted when you ask or
  when the project and its assessment end.
- **Voluntary:** donating is optional. You can withdraw a donation at any time by contacting me with
  the reference code shown after you submit.
- This work follows the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.
